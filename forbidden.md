# Forbidden zone

If a first viewport could be swapped onto another company by changing three nouns, it is slop.

These sites were produced by this skill. They are **fail specimens**. If your page looks like any of them, rebuild. Do not "fix" them by shipping the next cousin. Do not polish them instead of changing the skill.

### FOLD
Cousins, all fail: (1) sheet cropped or parked on the right edge (canvas CSS 2× viewport); (2) HUD on the mesh (FOLDO, PRINTT); (3) first frame is **empty cream** — camera `z` 8–10 on a small mesh, or WebGL died; (4) a visible **gray stage disk**; (5) crumple is a clay blob or a bent card; (6) a paper slider in leftover cream with **no designed air and no world change**, sold as the whole site. **Atmosphere and a transition are required. ### VEIL
Lessons already paid for. Do not rediscover them by polishing the glass card. Fail if any one hits: (1) mapped `PlaneGeometry` with no thickness; (2) dead-on rest pose, no yaw; (3) `transmission` washes the night face to **milky plastic**; (4) face is a matte brick (roughness ≥ 0.28, no specular / clearcoat); (5) dissolve samples random pixels or scatters more than 3% and the word smears; (6) offscreen `measureText` returns 0 and `fillText(wholeLine)` collapses the sentence. **Thickness + rest yaw + ink-dark dye + readable dissolve.**

### STRIKE

After the event: the cream becomes black, the crayon stays, two beige blobs appear on the stick. HUD whisper crops to a single letter. **A toy stick is KEPT. Height 25–40% with width ~2% is empty cream. A background hex swap is not a transition. Two blobs are not a dissolve.** Fail. Do not ship a match, pencil, nail, crayon, or capsule-on-end as the hero.

### INK

### CHIME
A smooth lathe pot on leftover cream. The profile never returns down the inner wall, so the well is closed and the inscribed line is invisible. ### SIGIL
An irregular wax blot with a circular gold stamp. Idle type can read (SIGIL in the ring) and the dye can stay oxblood. Still fail: (1) the limb is a **hard cutout** on flat gray-beige, FogExp2 / fresnel comments or not, so it is a sticker, not Augen still-life; (2) no thickness in the first frame, a spilled-ink puddle with three drip toes; (3) after the event the air goes night and a star-ring appears, but the **body stays a black puddle** and the word stays printed on it. **A readable stamp on a hard-cutout blot is a sticker. Haze must eat the limb. A leftover black puddle under a ring of dots is not a dissolve.** Fail. Do not ship an ink-blot / paw-blob / logo sticker as wax.

## Composition law (hard)

For object + control and material-transform first viewports:

1. The first frame shows the **whole object** with studio air around it. The object has **mass**: projected width ≥ 18% of the short axis, not a stick that is only tall.
2. FOV ~32. Camera `z` ~5.2–6.2 on a ~1.7 **wide** mesh. A 0.1-wide cylinder at `z` 5.5 is still empty cream (STRIKE). `z` 8–10 = empty cream.
3. Canvas CSS matches the viewport. A 2× style parks the mesh on the **right edge**.
4. HUD lives in the **top corners** and a control **below** the object. It never covers the mesh.
5. No gray stage disk.

Rooms you enter, shader fields, and spatial decks have their own scale. They still do not put a 工作台 card over the volume.

## Patterns that fail this skill

Hit any one and the page is not done.

- Centered badge + headline + sub + two CTAs (`max-w-3xl`).
- One headline word in a second color. Em-dash as flourish.
- Fake Mac / browser chrome, gray placeholder bars, dead lists.
- A letter about the product instead of the product.
- Dark rounded 工作台 card + textarea + yellow fill button.
- Feature rows with a hairline under each claim.
- Default Capsule / Sphere / Box / Cylinder+sphere head + `color.lerp` or a wood map that does not read (vitamin / STRIKE crayon).
- Match / pencil / nail / stick / crayon as the hero (no mass).
- Background hex swap while the mesh stays the same (not a transition).
- Dissolve that reads as two blobs or a cotton ball.
- Closed lathe / pot that hides the inscribed face (CHIME).
- Hard-cutout blot / ink-sticker with a circular stamp (SIGIL). Readable gold type does not save it.
- Leftover black puddle under a star ring after the event (SIGIL).
- A second disk under the mesh that reads as a plate (stage-disk cousin).
- Camera `z` 8–10 on a ~1.7 mesh (empty cream).
- Canvas CSS 2× viewport (object on the right edge).
- Cropped hero mesh. HUD colliding with type on the object.
- Gray `CircleGeometry` stage disk (unless the loop is a room).
- `transmission` > 0.06 on a mapped night face (milky plastic).
- Dissolve from random plane pixels, or scatter > 3% of glyph width (smear).
- `fillText(wholeLine)` after `measureText` returned 0 (collapsed type).
- A crumple slider as the whole site (no named air, no world change).
- Treating Augen as a night void. Treating Interfere as the bar.
- Inter / Roboto / system-ui as the only display voice.
- Elevate / seamless / unleash / transform your / unlock the power of.

## Fast test

Cover the logo. If you cannot tell what the company is, rewrite the first viewport.

Does the first screenshot show a complete object with designed air and **readable marks**, or a cropped poster / a form / a vitamin / a crayon in cream / empty cream / milky plastic / a hard-cutout sticker? If the latter, rebuild.
