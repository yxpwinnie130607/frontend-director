Hey, I’m Abhishek, a full-stack Product Designer with 3+ years of experience creating user-focused digital products across Web3, fintech, and gaming industries. I blend design thinking, technical know-how, and AI-powered workflows to move ideas from insight to impact.

My journey into design didn’t start in a classroom it started at a hackathon. While everyone around me was busy coding, I noticed something missing no one was thinking about the user experience. I had a background in graphics, so I started designing interfaces for my team. Soon, other teams began reaching out for help with their product flows and that’s when I realized design could shape how people experience technology.

Fast forward a few years, I’ve worked across industries like Fintech, Gaming, SportsTech, and Web3, helping fast-moving startups turn ambiguous ideas into products that find market fit, fast. Whether it’s building from scratch, designing for scale, or shaping MVPs, I’ve thrived in messy, high-speed environments.
