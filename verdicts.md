# Verdicts — where to go hard

Use this after the design read. Pick the row that matches the brief. The hard layer is a ceiling, not a shopping list.

## Product marketing / SaaS landing

**Audience:** buyers who already know the category. They need to feel the product, not be told it is "intelligent."

**May go hard:** a WebGL studio. One physical object (or a room, or a field). Pointer orbit. The job happens to the mesh. If it is one object: **whole object, studio air, HUD below** (Earth / Crumple).

**Must hold back:** invented illustration, bento-of-everything, three-tier pricing as the visual climax, logo walls inside the hero, a letter about the product, a fake window of gray bars, a 工作台 card.

**Aesthetic:** alive. Specific headline as a whisper. One accent. Motion is pointer / scroll / type on the volume.

**Wrong-hard:** a cinematic WebGL skin on top of a dead list. Also wrong: a beautiful still poster (素笺), a live 2D form (回声瓶), a vitamin capsule (KEPT), a cropped sheet (FOLD).

## Brand / studio / campaign site

**Audience:** clients, press, talent. They are hiring a point of view.

**May go hard:** one graphic idea taken to the wall: a room you enter, a field you dissolve into, or one object in a studio. The rest of the site is a straight corridor.

**Must hold back:** case-study cards that all look like Dribbble shots, agency clichés, a second climax after the first.

**Wrong-hard:** burst plus huge type plus coverflow plus scroll-hijack plus grain. Two climaxes cancel.

## Personal / designer / applicant portfolio

**Audience:** a human who will spend two minutes deciding if this person is specific.

**May go hard:** one 3D metaphor you can use, or a spatial deck of work. The person is the design.

**Must hold back:** chrome, "About / Work / Contact" templates, skill-bar charts, 12-project grids.

**Wrong-hard:** a student site that looks like an Awwwards agency reel.

## Agency portfolio (studio showing client work)

**May go hard:** a spatial deck, or work photographed large and sequenced.

**Must hold back:** capabilities lists as six icons, "we are a digital partner" filler, equal tiles.

## Editorial / magazine / essay

This skill still wants a volume if the brief is a site, not a PDF. A quiet letter as the hero is 素笺. Fail unless they asked for an essay and nothing else.

## E-commerce / product story

**May go hard:** the object is the design. Lighting, scale, dissolve, crumple. Whole object in a studio.

**Must hold back:** beige-and-brass artisan default, spec tables with a line under every row.

## Community / education / event

**May go hard:** one authored object or a room. Not a course-catalog card grid.

## Product app / dashboard / settings / docs

**Hard layer = none.** This skill mostly stands down. Do not drop a 工作台 marketing card on an app.

## Redesign of an existing site

**Preserve:** extract tokens, IA, and voice first. Hard layer may rise by one notch, not a full costume change.

**Overhaul:** treat visuals as greenfield, keep content and URLs.

If you cannot tell which, ask once: preserve the brand, or start visually from scratch?

## How "hard" is allowed to look

| Layer | Hard means | Hard does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| layout | One structural idea: studio, room, field | Every section a new grid stunt |
| type | One family; HUD is a whisper | A second display face for "emphasis" |
| material | One authored surface | Glass on every card, or a vitamin primitive |
| motion | Pointer / slider / type on the volume | Fade-up on every block |
| image | One real picture or one 3D object | Stock plus gradient orbs |
| composition | Whole object, air, HUD off the mesh | Cropped poster, overlapping type |

If two layers both feel necessary, keep the stronger one and demote the other to a whisper.
