## First viewport skeleton

Empty field. One small island (avatar + a pip, or a single verb). That is the page until they touch it.

## Required interaction

The island opens About / Links in a small sheet. Home restores the field.

## Do not

- Fill the field because it feels empty. Emptiness is the move.
- A marketing headline.
