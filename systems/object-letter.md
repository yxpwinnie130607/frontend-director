## First viewport skeleton

A one-column letter. Measure `min(62ch, 92vw)`. First image after two or three short paragraphs, large.

## Required interaction

Images can be advanced (click / arrows). The letter itself is the product. A buy action is a text link, not a floating pill over the hero.

## Do not

- Spec table with a line under every row.
- Beige + brass craft default.
- Feature cards.
