# Ceiling — sites that already did it

These were read on the live web, not from memory of "premium SaaS." Steal courage and structure. Do not clone skins.

The working picker is [archetypes.md](archetypes.md) — fourteen directions, pick one. This file is the longer reading for those sites.

The living ceiling is **Godly, now Recent**: https://recent.design. The homepage masonry (torus, flower, earth, glass, ice, paper) **is** the visual template: studio light, physical material, one object, depth. The Web filter adds motion (Liquid Glass, Koi, Black Hole, WebGL Portfolio). Use both. Do not flatten them into a form.

---

## Displace — https://displace.agency  (Recent: Websites, Interactive)

**Kind:** agency site that is a machine.

**What to steal:** the homepage *is* a desktop. Apps, notes, a terminal. You open, type, drag. The site does not explain the studio first.

**Do not steal:** the Mac skin, the traffic lights, the wallpaper. Steal "the first screen is usable."

---

## Offer Studio — https://offerstudio.group

**Kind:** brand / studio. User-named ceiling.

**What is actually good:**

- **Thesis:** one sentence, "Your website is your strongest offer," and nothing else in the first world.
- **Material:** a navy radial burst that is the entire room, not a background behind a card.
- **Type:** huge, centered, two weights. "strongest offer" goes softer so the line has a pulse. No eyebrow, no subcopy in that first world.
- **Motion:** a chapter change. The burst world dissolves into a white work stage. Theme flip is allowed because it is a door, not a random section invert.
- **Restraint after the climax:** once you are in the portfolio, chrome is a thin pill (`WORK / STORIES / OFFERSTUDIO / TEAM / CONTACT`). Work is shown as a designed object (a coverflow of real sites), not six equal thumbnails.
- **Why it is design:** they picked one idea and spent the whole first minute on it. The rest of the site is a hallway.

**Do not steal:** the burst itself, the coverflow, the pill nav, as a kit. Steal the willingness to let a first viewport contain only a sentence and a world.

## AndAgain — https://andagain.uk  (Godly: agency, large type, black & white)

**Kind:** agency.

**What is actually good:**

- **Type as architecture.** The wordmark is cropped by the viewport. The name is the building.
- **Layout:** a hairline, small meta on the left, a short manifesto on the right. No hero image. No card.
- **Whitespace:** most of the first screen is unused on purpose. That unused field is the luxury.
- **Restraint:** black, white, one size jump. Capabilities later are a list, not icons.

**Do not steal:** "crop a huge word" on a SaaS feature page. Steal the decision that type can be the only object.

## ped.ro — https://ped.ro  (Godly: personal, typographic, unusual layout)

**Kind:** personal portfolio.

**What is actually good:**

- **Type as interface.** The bio is the page. Names (`Pedro`, `Raycast`, `Barcelona`) are pills you can open. There is no project grid competing with the person.
- **Depth:** unread text sits behind a blur. Focus is a lighting choice, not a card border.
- **Voice:** "Yo! I'm Pedro" is specific. A hiring page that sounds like a template has already lost.
- **Restraint:** black field, almost no chrome, Home / Writing / Speaking / Shooting in a whisper.

**Do not steal:** blur-everything. Steal "the person is the information architecture."

---

## kons.fyi — https://kons.fyi  (Godly: personal, unusual layout, single page)

**Kind:** personal portfolio.

**What is actually good:**

- **Emptiness as the move.** A white field and one floating island (avatar + a green pip). The site is willing to look unfinished to people who want a grid.
- **Play:** the island is a toy, not a nav bar pretending to be fun.
- **Copy, when it arrives:** "value instead of mindless execution." The design is that sentence, enacted.

**Do not steal:** a floating pill on every site. Steal the nerve to ship almost nothing.

---

## Linear — https://linear.app  (Godly-class product marketing)

**Kind:** product marketing.

**What is actually good:**

- **Product-state is the hero.** A real workspace sits under a short, concrete headline. No gradient orb, no fake 3D blob, no "watch the product" poster frame.
- **Type:** large, but secondary to the tool. The sentence is specific ("The product development system for teams and agents"), not "Transform the way you work."
- **Chrome:** thin, dark, one pill for Sign up. The product screenshot has more visual weight than the marketing frame.
- **Restraint:** motion is in the product, not around it.

**Do not steal:** dark-mode-plus-screenshot as a default skin for every tool. Steal "show the thing."

---

## Amie — https://amie.so  (Godly: product, colourful, long scrolling)

**Kind:** product marketing, warmer than Linear.

**What is actually good:**

- Same structure as Linear (product in the hero) but the hard layer is friendliness: light ground, a blue pill, a headline that names a job ("Run your workday on autopilot with AI agents").
- Copy underneath is operational, not poetic. It says what the MCP does.
- Colour is one accent, not a rainbow.

**Do not steal:** "App Store featured" badges as a personality. Steal warmth that still shows the product.

---

## imjane.top — Offer Studio portfolio, Vassar applicant

**Kind:** personal / applicant portfolio.

**What is actually good:**

- **One metaphor.** A 3D apple tree on a pale hill. "Click the apples to explore." The whole site is a walk, not a resume PDF in a theme.
- **Image:** the tree is the identity. There is no header of skills.
- **Restraint:** almost no UI. Vertical "JANE'S PORTFOLIO" is the only label.
- **Why this is the Offer Studio lesson for portfolios:** personality first, spectacle only if it is *her* spectacle. A different applicant should not get the same tree.

**Do not steal:** the tree. Steal "one personal object, then stop."

## How to use Godly / Recent

Canonical URL: https://recent.design  
Old URL that still works: https://godly.website

This is a curated feed of *objects*, not only full websites. Top filters: All / Websites / Interfaces / Branding / Motion / Illustration / Posters / Print. Sidebar also splits Design, Websites, App screenshots, App icons.

When you need a fresh ceiling:

1. Filter **Web**. Open website posts, not 3D stills, posters, or illustration.
2. For each, write the loop: pointer / scroll / type / drag. That verb maps to `live-machine`, `kinetic-scroll`, or `pointer-world`.
3. Steal the loop. Leave the skin (no torus, no flower, no Mac wallpaper).
4. If the first viewport of your page cannot be used the way that Recent site can, rebuild.
